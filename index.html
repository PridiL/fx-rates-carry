Currencies by 2-year yield

Carry rank 1 = highest 2-year government yield. Changes in basis points over 5 / 20 / 65 trading days.

28 pairs: 2-year spread, its change, and the divergence signal

Spread = base 2y minus quote 2y, in percentage points. Spread rank 1 = the base out-yields the quote by the most. z move and z spread are the pair's 4-week move and the 4-week spread change, each divided by its own trailing 2-year standard deviation. A signal fires when they point opposite ways and both |z| ≥ 1; it bets the pair catches up with its spread over the next 20 trading days (the tested rule).
